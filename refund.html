Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: 27 April 2025  ·  Applies to all orders placed on BazaarLive

We want every purchase to feel like a win. If something isn't right, we'll make it right — within the terms below.

1. Return Window

You may request a return within 7 days of delivery for the following reasons:

  • Item received is significantly different from what was shown during the live stream.
  • Item is damaged, defective, or has a manufacturing fault.
  • Wrong item or wrong size delivered.
  • Item is missing from the package.

Returns are not accepted for change-of-mind, minor colour variations due to screen calibration, or items that have been used, washed, or altered.

2. Non-Returnable Items

  • Earrings and other pierced jewellery (hygiene reasons).
  • Innerwear and swimwear.
  • Items purchased under "Final Sale" or "Clearance" during a live stream (clearly announced by the seller).
  • Customised or made-to-order products.

3. How to Raise a Return Request

  1. Email manavb28@iimk.ac.in with subject "Return Request" and your Order ID in the message body
  2. Include photos or a short video clearly showing the issue.
  3. We will review and respond within 48 hours on business days.
  4. If approved, we will arrange a reverse pickup at no cost to you.

4. Refund Process

Once the returned item is received and quality-checked (up to 3 business days), your refund will be processed:

  • Original payment method — UPI, debit/credit card: refunded within 5–7 business days after approval.
  • BazaarLive Wallet Credit (optional) — instant credit, redeemable on next order.

Razorpay processing timelines may vary by bank. Shipping charges (if any) are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

5. Order Cancellation

  • You may cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it, before it is dispatched. Email support immediately.
  • Once dispatched, cancellation is not possible — you may initiate a return after delivery.
  • BazaarLive may cancel orders due to stock unavailability, pricing errors, or suspected fraud. Full refunds are issued in such cases within 3–5 business days.

6. Damaged in Transit

If your package arrives visibly damaged, please photograph it before opening and email us within 24 hours of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no charge.

7. Dispute Escalation

If your return or refund request is not resolved to your satisfaction within 10 business days, you may escalate to our Grievance Officer or approach the National Consumer Helpline (1800-11-4000).

8. Contact
