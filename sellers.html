How It Works

From Market Stall to Your Cart — Live

A seamless three-step journey for buyers and a powerful broadcast tool for sellers.

📲

Seller Goes Live

Verified sellers open the BazaarLive Seller App and start a 2-hour live session. They showcase products, answer buyer questions, and set prices in real time.

🛍️

Buyers Watch & Buy

Shoppers open the Buyer App, browse live streams from trusted Sarojini sellers, and tap to add items to cart — without ever leaving the stream.

🚚

Order & Deliver

Payment is collected instantly at checkout. Orders are tracked through the Ops Console and fulfilled by the seller or via manual logistics coordination.

💸

Seller Gets Paid

Settlements are processed within 2 business days directly to the seller's registered bank account via Razorpay — transparent, trackable, and on time.

What We Offer

Everything You Need to Sell Live

BazaarLive is a full-stack live commerce platform purpose-built for Indian street and boutique sellers.

📹

Live Video Streaming

Low-latency HD video powered by Agora. Sellers broadcast and interact with hundreds of viewers simultaneously with <500ms delay.

🛒

In-Stream Checkout

One-tap product purchase overlaid on the live video. No context switch — buyers never leave the stream to complete a purchase.

💳

Secure Payments Razorpay

UPI, cards, net banking, and wallets accepted. Payments are collected, held, and settled to sellers via Razorpay's Payment Gateway.

📊

Seller Dashboard

Track live session performance, order history, revenue, and pending settlements from the Seller App or the Ops Console.

🔔

Buyer OTP Auth

Frictionless phone-number sign-in for buyers via SMS OTP. No passwords, no email — just a mobile number and you're in.

🏪

Seller Onboarding

Curated invite-only marketplace. Every seller is verified by the BazaarLive team before going live to ensure product quality and trust.
