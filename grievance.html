As required by the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020

If you have a complaint about our services, content on our platform, or handling of your personal data, you may contact our designated Grievance Officer directly. We are committed to resolving all complaints within 30 days of receipt.

What to Include in Your Complaint

To help us resolve your issue promptly, please include:

  • Your registered mobile number.
  • Order ID (if complaint relates to a purchase).
  • A clear description of the issue.
  • Supporting evidence — screenshots, photos, or video where applicable.
  • Your preferred resolution (refund, replacement, content removal, etc.).

Types of Complaints We Handle

  • Unresolved order, delivery, or refund issues.
  • Misleading or inaccurate product representation by a seller.
  • Privacy or data protection concerns.
  • Unlawful, abusive, or inappropriate content in live streams or chats.
  • Account access issues or unauthorised transactions.
  • Any other grievance relating to BazaarLive's services.

External Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our resolution, you may approach:

  • National Consumer Helpline — 1800-11-4000 (toll-free) or consumerhelpline.gov.in
  • Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
  • Cyber Crime Portal — cybercrime.gov.in for fraud or data misuse.
