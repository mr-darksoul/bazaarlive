Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 May 2026  ·  Effective: 27 April 2025

BazaarLive ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can exercise your rights under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Phone number — required for OTP-based sign-in.
  • Delivery address — collected at checkout to fulfil your order.
  • Payment information — we do not store card or UPI details. All payment data is handled directly by Razorpay (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified).

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • IP address and approximate location (city/state level).
  • Session logs: pages viewed, streams watched, products tapped, time spent.
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics.
  • Device identifiers — the BazaarLive app requests the READ_PHONE_STATE Android permission solely to allow Razorpay to read a device identifier (e.g. IMEI or equivalent) for payment fraud detection and risk scoring. This identifier is transmitted to Razorpay and is never stored by BazaarLive independently. See Razorpay's privacy policy at razorpay.com/privacy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To process orders and arrange delivery.
  • To send order confirmations and delivery updates via SMS.
  • To personalise your live-stream feed and product recommendations.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and policy violations.
  • To comply with applicable Indian law and respond to lawful government requests.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Razorpay Software Private Limited — to process payments securely, including device identifiers for fraud prevention as described in §1.2.
  • Logistics partners — your name and delivery address, solely to fulfil your order.
  • Agora Inc. — for real-time video streaming infrastructure (no personal data shared beyond session tokens).
  • Supabase Inc. — for authentication, database, and backend hosting (data stored in AWS Mumbai region).
  • Legal authorities — when required by a court order or applicable law.

4. Android App — Google Play Data Safety

In compliance with Google Play's Data Safety requirements, the following summarises data collected by the BazaarLive app:

  • Personal info — phone number (required, shared with Razorpay and Supabase).
  • Financial info — purchase history (required, stored for order fulfilment).
  • Device or other IDs — device identifier via READ_PHONE_STATE (required, shared with Razorpay for fraud prevention only; not used for advertising).
  • App activity — in-app actions, crash logs (required, used for app improvement).

No data is collected for advertising or marketing profiling. All data collection is required for core app functionality.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active and for up to 3 years thereafter, or as required by Indian tax and commercial law. Order records are retained for 7 years to comply with GST regulations.

6. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of your data (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential data processing.
  • Nominate a representative to exercise these rights on your behalf.

To exercise any right, email manavb28@iimk.ac.in with subject line "Data Request". We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses only essential cookies (session management). We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Security

All data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.2+. Data at rest is stored on Supabase (hosted in AWS Mumbai) with AES-256 encryption. We conduct periodic security reviews and limit data access to authorised personnel only.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of BazaarLive after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact our Grievance Officer at grievance.html or email manavb28@iimk.ac.in.
